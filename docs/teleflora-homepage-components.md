# Teleflora Homepage Reusable Components

Analysis date: April 27, 2026

The live Teleflora homepage is built from repeatable commerce and merchandising patterns. The prototype now includes matching reusable modules that can later be driven by CMS, commerce, search, or delivery availability APIs.

## Components Created

- `Header` and `Navigation`: utility links, phone/order/cart affordances, and commerce category navigation.
- `QuickShop`: occasion, delivery date, and ZIP capture for high-intent shoppers.
- `OccasionShortcuts`: image-led links for Same Day, Birthday, Mother's Day, Thank You, Get Well, and Sympathy.
- `ProductRail`: repeatable product sections for holiday collections and occasion-led selling.
- `CampaignFeature`: large image-led promotional band for seasonal storytelling.
- `FeaturedCategories`: collection cards for broader category discovery.
- `PromoBanner`: compact value-prop cards for delivery, campaign, and florist-network messaging.
- `EditorialContent`: SEO content and FAQ structure.
- `NewsletterSignup`: email capture with concise value props.
- `Footer`: dense multi-column footer link groups and trust messaging.

## Homepage Patterns Observed

- A commerce header with international delivery, phone number, login/cart/order status, and category navigation.
- A quick-shop area that asks for occasion, delivery date, and recipient ZIP.
- A seasonal hero centered on the current campaign.
- Occasion shortcut tiles for common shopper intents.
- Multiple product rails with repeated card behavior, price visibility, and buy-now CTAs.
- Image-led campaign storytelling between product sections.
- SEO copy, FAQ content, newsletter signup, trust links, and a dense footer.
